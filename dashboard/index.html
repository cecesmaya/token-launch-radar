How to read this board. A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama's record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it. That is DefiLlama's record, not proof: a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued, and an implied value is this radar's own claim, never make.fun's. Farm only money you can afford to lose, and never pay anyone to claim an airdrop — a real claim costs no fee beyond gas.
